# Code of Conduct

HackStorm 2027 is committed to a safe, welcoming, and harassment-free environment for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and inclusive.
- Collaborate openly and give credit where due.
- Respect shared spaces, equipment, and quiet/sleeping areas.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, or threats in any form.
- Plagiarism or misrepresenting others' work.
- Malware, illegal content, or misuse of personal data.

## Reporting

Report any incident immediately to a volunteer or Code of Conduct officer, or via the Discord #report channel. Violations may result in removal from the event without refund or prize eligibility.
